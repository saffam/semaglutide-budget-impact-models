# Model Assumptions

This document describes the key assumptions used in the semaglutide budget impact models.

---

# General Modelling Framework

Each country model follows a five-year budget impact approach:

1. Estimate target population
2. Apply obesity prevalence
3. Estimate eligible treatment population
4. Apply treatment uptake scenarios
5. Calculate medication costs and healthcare offsets

---

# Treatment Uptake Assumptions

The models apply gradual adoption scenarios.

Example:

| Year | Uptake |
|---|---|
| Year 1 | 5% |
| Year 5 | 25% |

These values represent hypothetical adoption scenarios for budget impact estimation.

---

# Treatment Cost Assumptions

Annual treatment cost is calculated as:

Annual Cost per Patient × Number of Treated Patients

Country-specific medication pricing is applied.

---

# Healthcare Cost Offset Assumptions

Healthcare savings represent potential reductions in obesity-associated healthcare expenditure.

The model assumes:

Healthcare Savings = Treated Population × Estimated Cost Offset per Patient

---

# Important Limitations

The models are designed as exploratory budget impact analyses.

They do not represent:

- Clinical effectiveness modelling
- Cost-effectiveness analysis
- Individual patient outcomes
- Real-world prescribing forecasts

Results depend on assumptions regarding:

- Treatment uptake
- Medication pricing
- Eligibility criteria
- Healthcare savings estimates

All assumptions can be modified for alternative scenarios.
