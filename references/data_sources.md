# Data Sources

This project uses only publicly available healthcare datasets and published evidence.

All datasets are selected to support transparent and reproducible Health Economics and Outcomes Research (HEOR) modelling.

---

# United Kingdom 🇬🇧

## Population Data

Source:
Office for National Statistics (ONS)

Purpose:
- Adult population estimates
- Population denominators for obesity modelling
 
---

## Obesity Prevalence

Source:
Health Survey for England (HSE)

Purpose:
- Estimate obesity prevalence among adults
- Calculate eligible treatment population

---

## Healthcare Context

Sources:
- NHS England
- Published healthcare cost studies

Purpose:
- Inform healthcare system assumptions
- Support budget impact interpretation

---

## Obesity Prevalence

## Medication Costs

Source:

Purpose:

Source:

Purpose:
- Estimate potential healthcare savings
